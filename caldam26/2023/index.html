CALDAM 2023

(the 9th International Conference on
Algorithms and Discrete Applied Mathematics)


is being held between February 9– 11, 2023 at
DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India.

The conference is organised by the

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of
Information & Communication Technology
(DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, Gujarat,

(in association with IIT Gandhinagar)
 

& the Association for Computer Science and Discrete Mathematics (ACSDM), India.

The program committee consisted of
30 highly experienced and active researchers
from various countries.
