CALDAM 2026

(the 12th International Conference on
Algorithms and Discrete Applied Mathematics)


is being held between February 12–14, 2026 at
Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad, India.

The conference is organised by the

Department of Mathematics,
Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad
Karnataka, India

& the Association for Computer Science and Discrete Mathematics (ACSDM), India.

The program committee consisted of
28 highly experienced and active researchers
from various countries.
